## What Happened

Eurozone PMI Composite rebounded to **51.6** in the latest reading, driven by manufacturing's strongest expansion since **2022**, signalling a decisive break above the 50.0 neutral threshold and reversing prior contraction fears.

## What It Means

This reacceleration in activity eases stagflation concerns and reshapes the ECB's policy calculus—a sustained PMI above 51 typically signals durable demand, potentially delaying rate cuts and supporting the euro. For cyclicals and industrials, the transmission is direct: **Siemens**, **Alstom**, and **ThyssenKrupp** (DAX) benefit immediately from order books strengthening; **Renault**, **Stellantis**, and **Michelin** (CAC 40) see margin expansion as factory utilization normalizes; **ASML** (AEX) and semiconductor suppliers gain from capex acceleration in manufacturing-heavy sectors. The PMI gains also imply lower equity risk premiums for capital-intensive firms, supporting re-rating of beaten-down industrials relative to defensive utilities and REITs.

## Who Is Affected

Asset managers running cyclical and value-heavy portfolios—particularly those overweight DAX and CAC 40 industrials—now face reduced downside hedging costs and improved timing for tactical overweighting. Corporates in auto supply chains and machinery manufacturing see inventory drawdowns and forward guidance upgrades within reach; workers in manufacturing hubs across Germany and northern France face improved hiring visibility.
