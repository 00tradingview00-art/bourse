## What Happened

ECB President Christine Lagarde reaffirmed the central bank's commitment to the current restrictive rate path, signaling no imminent pivot to cuts despite softer recent economic data.

## What It Means

Lagarde's hawkish messaging extends the duration of elevated discount rates across European equity markets. For **banks** like **ING**, **Deutsche Bank**, and **BNP Paribas**, sustained higher rates support net interest margin expansion — a structural tailwind offsetting loan growth concerns. Conversely, this prolongs pressure on **REITs** and **Utilities**, where dividend yields face competition from risk-free rates above **4%**, compressing price-to-book multiples. Growth-exposed names across the **DAX** and **CAC 40** — including **SAP** and industrials — face extended valuation headwinds as the cost of capital remains elevated. The signal also tightens refinancing conditions for leveraged corporates and property developers, weighing on **Vonovia** and **LEG Immobilien**.

## Who Is Affected

Institutional investors managing rate-sensitive allocations — pension funds, insurance groups, and fixed-income managers — must rebalance away from cyclical growth toward defensive yielders and financial stocks. European households and SMEs refinancing debt face higher borrowing costs, dampening consumption and capex cycles through **Q4 2024**.
