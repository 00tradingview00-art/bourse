## What Happened

ECB Governing Council member Makhlouf stated that each monetary policy meeting, including October, remains "live"—signalling the central bank has not pre-committed to a rate path and will reassess data on inflation, growth, and employment before each decision.

## What It Means

This statement sustains **rate uncertainty**, which compresses valuation multiples for cyclical sectors while simultaneously lifting defensive positioning. For **European banks** (**ING**, **Santander**, **BNP Paribas**, **Crédit Agricole**), sustained higher-for-longer rates support net interest margins, but terminal rate ambiguity delays equity repricing upward. Conversely, **growth stocks** and **software firms** (**SAP**, **Siemens**) face extended discount-rate pressure if the ECB remains hawkish into Q4. **REITs and Utilities** (German DAX utilities, French energy plays) remain pinned by elevated financing costs, with no clear refinancing relief signalled.

## Who Is Affected

Asset allocators managing pan-European equity mandates face recalibrated sector hedging decisions; passive trackers in DAX, CAC 40, and FTSE 100 will reflect rotation away from rate-sensitive growth into financials. Retail investors and SMEs refinancing debt in October face unchanged ECB terminal rates, deferring capital deployment.
