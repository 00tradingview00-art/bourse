## Opening

European equities advanced broadly on Wednesday, with the IBEX 35 leading regional gains at +0.85% to close at 19,195.7, while the DAX and FTSE 100 matched each other at +0.71%, reaching 25,265.5 and 10,626.7 respectively. The IBEX 35's outperformance is the session's most significant move, reinforcing Spain's position as one of the stronger-performing major European markets and reflecting continued investor confidence in Iberian financials, which carry substantial index weight. On the currency side, the euro softened 0.22% against the dollar to 1.1181, a modest headwind for eurozone exporters but one that provides a partial offset for European investors with dollar-denominated holdings.

Brent crude rose 1.95% to $106.76, adding pressure to European energy-intensive sectors and threatening to widen the continent's import bill at a time when inflation remains a central bank concern. Gold slipped 0.52% to $4,194.30, suggesting a modest rotation away from safe-haven assets that may provide marginal support for risk appetite in European equities.

## Key stock move

Adyen (AEX) was the session's standout mover, rising 2.28% to €906.30, as the Amsterdam-listed payments group continued to recover investor confidence following its 2023 growth scare. The gain outpaced advances in Iberdrola, SAP, Shell, and Siemens, which added between 0.85% and 1.55%.
